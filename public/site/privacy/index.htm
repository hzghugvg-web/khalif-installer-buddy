Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 21, 2026

KINST provides iOS IPA installation, signing, certificate, DNS, и related tools и information. This Privacy Policy explains how information may be processed when you use KINST.

Files Submitted to the IPA Signer

When you use the KINST IPA Signer, files you submit may include IPA files, P12 signing certificates, и mobile provisioning profiles. These files are temporarily processed on the KINST server only as necessary to perform the requested signing operation.

Certificate passwords submitted through the public signer are used during the signing request и are not intentionally stored in the public signer database.

Temporary File Retention

Uploaded files и generated signing files are intended to remain available only temporarily. Normal successful signing requests use short-lived temporary storage, и KINST also uses automated cleanup processes that target stale temporary и signed files older than approximately 30 minutes.

Temporary files may remain longer in unusual situations such as interrupted processes, server errors, or maintenance.

Technical и Server Information

Like most websites, KINST may process stиard technical information including IP addresses, browser or device information, timestamps, requested URLs, и server logs. This information may be used for security, troubleshooting, abuse prevention, analytics, и improving the service.

Third-Party Services

KINST may link to or use third-party services including YouTube, Discord, Telegram, software repositories, download providers, advertising services, и other external websites.

Those services operate under their own privacy policies и practices. KINST does not control how third-party websites process information.

Sale of Personal Information

KINST does not intentionally sell uploaded signing files or personal information submitted through the IPA signing process.

Your Responsibility

Do not submit sensitive files unless they are necessary for the requested service. You should only upload certificates, applications, и other files that you are authorized to use.

Changes to This Policy

KINST may update this Privacy Policy when the website, signing service, or related features change. The latest version will be published on this page.

Contact

For questions about this Privacy Policy, you can contact KINST through the official Discord or Telegram links available on the KINST website.
