Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 21, 2026

AppleJr provides iOS IPA installation, signing, certificate, DNS, and related tools and information. This Privacy Policy explains how information may be processed when you use AppleJr.

Files Submitted to the IPA Signer

When you use the AppleJr IPA Signer, files you submit may include IPA files, P12 signing certificates, and mobile provisioning profiles. These files are temporarily processed on the AppleJr server only as necessary to perform the requested signing operation.

Certificate passwords submitted through the public signer are used during the signing request and are not intentionally stored in the public signer database.

Temporary File Retention

Uploaded files and generated signing files are intended to remain available only temporarily. Normal successful signing requests use short-lived temporary storage, and AppleJr also uses automated cleanup processes that target stale temporary and signed files older than approximately 30 minutes.

Temporary files may remain longer in unusual situations such as interrupted processes, server errors, or maintenance.

Technical and Server Information

Like most websites, AppleJr may process standard technical information including IP addresses, browser or device information, timestamps, requested URLs, and server logs. This information may be used for security, troubleshooting, abuse prevention, analytics, and improving the service.

Third-Party Services

AppleJr may link to or use third-party services including YouTube, Discord, Telegram, software repositories, download providers, advertising services, and other external websites.

Those services operate under their own privacy policies and practices. AppleJr does not control how third-party websites process information.

Sale of Personal Information

AppleJr does not intentionally sell uploaded signing files or personal information submitted through the IPA signing process.

Your Responsibility

Do not submit sensitive files unless they are necessary for the requested service. You should only upload certificates, applications, and other files that you are authorized to use.

Changes to This Policy

AppleJr may update this Privacy Policy when the website, signing service, or related features change. The latest version will be published on this page.

Contact

For questions about this Privacy Policy, you can contact AppleJr through the official Discord or Telegram links available on the AppleJr website.
